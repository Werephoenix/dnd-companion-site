How much can this device keep?

This is a one-off check for WereWick. It finds out how much your browser lets the game save on this device.

Before you tap: close the game if it is open in another tab. The check takes about ten seconds, removes everything it wrote, and sends nothing anywhere. It does not touch your heroes or your saves.

And the picture store?

Pictures you add yourself are kept in a second, roomier store. This checks how much it will take on this device.

It writes up to 40 MB of blank test data into a store of its own, then deletes that store. It never touches the game's pictures and sends nothing anywhere. About fifteen seconds.
